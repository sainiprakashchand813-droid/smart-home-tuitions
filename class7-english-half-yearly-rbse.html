HALF-YEARLY EXAMINATION 2026–27

ENGLISH – CLASS VII (RBSE Practice Set)

Time: 3 Hours | Maximum Marks: 80

General Instructions:

  1. All questions are compulsory unless an internal choice is given.
  2. Read each question carefully and write in neat, simple English.
  3. Marks are indicated against each question.
  4. Literature questions are based on the practice chapters listed in Section D.

Section A – Reading (15 Marks)

Q1. Read the passage and answer the questions. (8 × 1 = 8)

Water is one of the most precious gifts of nature. Every living being needs water to survive. We use it for drinking, cooking, cleaning and growing crops. However, clean water is becoming scarce in many places because of pollution, wastage and falling groundwater levels. We can save water by closing taps properly, repairing leaking pipes, collecting rainwater and reusing water whenever possible. Students can also spread awareness in their homes and schools. If everyone takes small steps every day, a large amount of water can be saved for the future.

  1. Why is water important?
  2. Mention any two uses of water.
  3. Give two reasons why clean water is becoming scarce.
  4. Write one way to save water at home.
  5. What can students do to help?
  6. Find a word in the passage that means “not plentiful”.
  7. Write the opposite of “waste” as a verb.
  8. Give a suitable title to the passage.
Q2. Read the information and answer. (7 × 1 = 7)

Green Valley School organised a book fair for three days. On the first day, 120 students visited it; on the second day, 150 students visited it; and on the third day, 180 students visited it. The school library bought 45 storybooks and 30 science books. The money collected from the sale of old newspapers was used to buy plants for the school garden.

  1. How many days did the book fair last?
  2. How many students visited on the first day?
  3. On which day did the highest number of students visit?
  4. How many storybooks did the library buy?
  5. How many science books did it buy?
  6. What was bought with the money from old newspapers?
  7. Suggest one benefit of organising a book fair.

Section B – Writing (15 Marks)

Q3. (5 marks)

Write a letter to your friend describing your school’s annual function. (80–100 words)

OR Write an application to your Principal requesting leave for three days because you are ill.

Q4. (5 marks)

Write a paragraph in 80–100 words on “My Favourite Teacher”.

OR “Importance of Trees”.

Q5. (5 marks)

Write a notice for your school notice board about a cleanliness drive to be held in the school. Include date, time, place and instructions.

OR Write a short story in 80–100 words beginning: “One morning, I found a small puppy near my school…”

Section C – Grammar (15 Marks)

Q6. Fill in the blanks with suitable articles/prepositions. (5 × 1 = 5)
  1. She is ___ intelligent girl.
  2. The cat is sitting ___ the chair.
  3. We have lived here ___ 2021.
  4. Rohan goes to school ___ bus.
  5. The sun rises in ___ east.
Q7. Choose the correct verb form. (5 × 1 = 5)
  1. My brother ___ (play/plays/playing) cricket every evening.
  2. They ___ (was/were/is) happy yesterday.
  3. I ___ (am/is/are) reading a story now.
  4. She ___ (has/have/having) a blue bag.
  5. We ___ (will visit/visited/visits) Jaipur tomorrow.
Q8. Do as directed. (5 × 1 = 5)
  1. He is a very kind boy. (Change into exclamatory sentence)
  2. Rita writes a letter. (Change into past tense)
  3. The teacher helps the students. (Change into passive voice)
  4. This is a beautiful flower. (Change into plural)
  5. You are a student, ___? (Add a question tag)

Section D – Literature (35 Marks)

Practice selection: Honeycomb – Three Questions, A Gift of Chappals, Gopal and the Hilsa-Fish; poems – The Squirrel, The Rebel, The Shed. Supplementary reader – The Tiny Teacher. Please confirm these chapters with your school.

Q9. Answer any five in 30–40 words. (5 × 2 = 10)
  1. What were the three questions that the king wanted answered in “Three Questions”?
  2. Why did the king help the wounded man?
  3. Why did Mridu go to the backyard in “A Gift of Chappals”?
  4. How did Gopal manage to enter the palace?
  5. What does the squirrel do in the poem “The Squirrel”?
  6. What is a rebel according to the poem “The Rebel”?
  7. What makes ants disciplined and hardworking?
Q10. Answer any three in 60–80 words. (3 × 3 = 9)
  1. How did the hermit answer the king’s three questions?
  2. Describe the main events of “A Gift of Chappals”.
  3. How did Gopal prove that he was clever?
  4. Describe the life and habits of ants as given in “The Tiny Teacher”.
Q11. Reference to context – Poetry. (2 × 3 = 6)

Read these original practice lines: “A little squirrel climbs the tree, / And chatters happily at me.”

  1. Name the poem related to a squirrel.
  2. Which animal is described in the lines?
  3. Write one characteristic of the animal shown in the poem.
Q12. Answer any two in about 80–100 words. (2 × 5 = 10)
  1. Explain the message of “Three Questions”.
  2. Write a character sketch of Gopal.
  3. Describe the organisation and teamwork in an ant colony.

Section B – Writing

Q3 – Sample informal letter:

12, Shastri Nagar, Jaipur
23 November 2026

Dear Neha,
I hope you are fine. I want to tell you about our school’s annual function. It was held last Friday in the school auditorium. The programme began with a prayer and welcome speech. Students presented dances, songs and a short play. Our Principal gave prizes to students for their achievements. My class performed a group song and everyone appreciated it. The school was beautifully decorated. I enjoyed the function very much and wished you were there.

Yours lovingly,
Riya

Q4 – My Favourite Teacher: My favourite teacher is Mrs Sharma, who teaches us English. She is kind, patient and helpful. She explains every lesson in simple words and encourages us to ask questions. She never makes fun of students who make mistakes. She also teaches us discipline, honesty and respect for others. She gives interesting activities and helps weak students after class. I enjoy her lessons because she makes learning easy and enjoyable. I want to become a good person like her.

OR – Importance of Trees: Trees are very important for life. They give us oxygen, fruits, flowers and shade. They provide homes to birds and animals and keep the air clean. Their roots hold the soil and help prevent soil erosion. Trees also make our surroundings beautiful and cool. We should not cut trees unnecessarily. Every student should plant a tree and take care of it. Saving trees means saving our environment and our future.

OR – Sample story: One morning, I found a small puppy near my school. It looked hungry and frightened. I gave it some water and shared my lunch with it. I informed my class teacher, who helped me contact an animal rescue group. The volunteers came and checked the puppy. They took it to a safe shelter and promised to find it a loving home. I felt happy that I had helped a helpless animal. I learned that kindness towards animals is our responsibility.

Section D – Literature

Q9 – Short answers:

  1. The king wanted to know the right time to begin everything, the most important people to listen to, and the most important thing to do.
  2. The king saw a wounded man and felt compassion for him. He washed and bandaged the man’s wound and gave him water. He helped him because a human being in pain needed care.
  3. Mridu heard a strange noise and went to the backyard to find out what was happening. There she discovered a kitten hidden in a basket and learned about the children’s plan to help it.
  4. Gopal dressed himself in an unusual way, covering himself with ash and wearing strange clothes. People laughed at his appearance and did not recognise him. This helped him enter the palace and meet the king.
  5. The squirrel runs up and down the tree, sits on a branch and eats nuts. It is active, playful and quick.
  6. A rebel is someone who likes to do the opposite of what others are doing. The rebel behaves differently from the crowd, sometimes simply to be different.
  7. Ants work in an organised way, follow duties, cooperate with one another and store food. Their discipline and teamwork help the colony survive.

Q10 – Long answers:

  1. The hermit explained that the most important time is the present, because it is the only time we can act. The most important person is the one we are with, because we can help that person now. The most important thing is to do good to that person. The story teaches us to live mindfully and kindly.
  2. In “A Gift of Chappals”, Mridu visits her cousins and discovers a kitten hidden in the backyard. The children feel sorry for it and give it milk. When a beggar arrives with bare feet, the children secretly give him the music teacher’s chappals. This creates confusion when the teacher cannot find his footwear. The story shows the children’s kindness as well as their childish decision-making.
  3. Gopal proved his cleverness by accepting the king’s challenge to buy a hilsa-fish and bring it to the palace without anyone talking about the fish. He dressed in a funny and unusual manner, so people were more interested in his appearance than in the fish. He successfully reached the king and demonstrated his intelligence and sense of humour.
  4. Ants live in colonies and work together in an organised manner. Different ants perform different duties, such as caring for young ones, collecting food, guarding the nest and looking after the queen. They communicate and cooperate with one another. Their hard work, discipline and teamwork help the whole colony remain safe and active.

Q11 – Poetry: (1) “The Squirrel”. (2) A squirrel. (3) It is lively, quick, playful and active.

Q12 – Extended answers:

  1. “Three Questions” teaches that we should focus on the present moment, care for the person who is with us and do good whenever we can. The king learns that the future is uncertain and that the best way to use time is to act wisely now. Kindness and service are more valuable than simply knowing answers to difficult questions. The story encourages us to be thoughtful, helpful and compassionate in everyday life.
  2. Gopal is clever, confident and humorous. He accepts the king’s difficult challenge and thinks of an unusual plan to complete it. He changes his appearance so much that people focus on him instead of discussing the fish. He remains calm and uses his intelligence to reach the palace. His actions show quick thinking, creativity and courage.
  3. An ant colony is organised and works like a small community. The queen lays eggs, workers collect food and care for the young, and soldiers protect the nest. Each ant performs its duty and cooperates with others. Ants communicate through chemical signals and work together to find food and maintain the colony. Their teamwork, discipline and hard work teach us the value of cooperation.
